# Your own

A new application is a folder with a window that has nothing on it and a specification with
nothing in it yet. Everything about it is in the RulealizeStudio.Avalonia view, in the activity
bar: its screen, its specification, and its test cases once it has rules.

Its screen opens as its window, drawn as it will be: drag the controls its build loads onto it,
where you want them, move them, size them by their corner, delete them and give them words, without
reading XAML. It can have more windows, and more specifications: the + on **Screens** and on
**Specifications** in the view adds one, and the bin on a line deletes it. When a window appears and goes is said in a specification, and bound
to the rules like the rest. What you write of its screen
and its specification is yours; commit it where you commit anything. When your agent writes its
rules from them, the view says under the screen and the specification what changed of what you
made beyond binding it to the rules. Once it has rules, it goes as the example does: read its
test cases, ask your agent for a change where you see one, and read what it did.
